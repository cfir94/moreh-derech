## הפרומפט (באנגלית, כך Codex מקבל אותו)

> Read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/team/PROTOCOL.md` and `PRODUCT.md` (section "Brand Commitments") first.
>
> **Context.** The site "אבן דרך למורי דרך" ("Milestone for tour guides") serves Israeli tour-guide students and working guides, aged 40–60. The owner wants it to feel like something a guide pulled out of his backpack mid-tour: a map, a field notebook, a teaching aid. Earth palette: light browns, dark browns, greens (soil, sand, vegetation). Nothing that looks generic or AI-made: no glossy gradients, no emoji style, no generic "app icon in a rounded square" look. Two worlds are being tested: **(A)** Israel's brown heritage/tourist-site road signs and national-park interpretation boards; **(B)** Israeli trail blazes (painted white–colour–white stripes on rocks) and the 1:50,000 hiking trail map with contour lines.
>
> **Deliverable.** Three complete variations, each a coherent system drawn by one hand. Write them as hand-authored SVG (no raster, no embedded images, no fonts, no `<text>`). Put everything under `design/explore/cx07/`:
>
> - **V1 — "Heritage sign" (world A).** Pictograms like the white symbols on Israel's brown tourist signs: solid filled shapes, sturdy, readable at 24px, cut-out details. Drawn to sit as cream/white on a brown sign plate.
> - **V2 — "Painted blaze" (world B).** Icons that look painted with a brush on limestone: slightly uneven stroke ends, the trail-blaze stripe as a recurring device, contour-line hints.
> - **V3 — "Field notebook ink" (both worlds).** A guide's ink sketch in a field notebook: single-weight pen line (about 1.75px at 24px), hand-drawn wobble kept subtle, one hatching style for shadow.
>
> For **each** variation `vN/`:
> 1. `logo-mark.svg` (viewBox `0 0 64 64`): the brand mark for "אבן דרך". Build it from a **Roman milestone** (a short stone column on a base, as found along Roman roads in the Land of Israel) merged with one element of that variation's world (V1: the sign plate; V2: a white–colour–white blaze painted on the milestone; V3: an ink sketch with a road/path line). It must read at 32px. No letters inside the mark.
> 2. Twelve icons, viewBox `0 0 24 24`, one file each, named exactly: `quizzes.svg` (question bank), `exams.svg` (licensing exam, timed), `map.svg` (learning map), `timelines.svg` (timelines / learning aids), `games.svg`, `videos.svg`, `notes.svg` (personal notes: a notebook page), `review.svg` (review my mistakes), `community.svg` (class forum), `me.svg` (personal area / progress), `notebooks.svg` (knowledge bases for tours), `guide-reports.svg` (tour reports). Each icon must be a field object a guide actually carries or meets on a tour where possible (compass, folded map, field notebook, binoculars, water canteen, signpost, milestone, trail blaze, potsherd, hat), not generic UI glyphs (no generic gear, no generic chat bubble, no generic play triangle in a circle, no generic person bust).
> 3. Use `currentColor` for the main ink, and at most one secondary colour through `var(--icon-accent, <fallback hex>)`, so the site can theme them for light and dark mode.
> 4. Consistency rules inside a variation: same stroke width (or same fill logic), same corner treatment, same optical size, same level of detail. Across variations they must be clearly different at thumbnail size.
> 5. `spot.svg` (viewBox `0 0 320 200`): one small illustration showing the variation's **image style**, a guide's open backpack with a folded map coming out of it, in the earth palette (up to 5 flat colours: sand `#D9C7A3`, limestone `#E9DFCB`, sign brown `#5B3A21`, terebinth green `#4F6B3A`, basalt `#2A221B`; V2 may add trail red `#B83A2B` and trail blue `#2C5C8A`).
>
> Then add `design/explore/cx07/index.html`: a static board that shows the three variations side by side, each icon at 24px and 48px, on a light sand ground and on a dark basalt ground (`#1F1A15`), with the logo mark at 32px and 128px, and the spot illustration. Labels in Hebrew are fine. It must open directly from disk (relative paths, inline `<img>` or `<object>`), with no build step.
>
