# Reigns and offices — Roman people (25 October 2026)

Canonical supplemental data: src/data/roman-tenures.ts. Every one of the 36 original people and three existing Herodian companions has a role-appropriate entry. The original event dates, actions, comparisons and portrait data are unchanged.

Each entry distinguishes an event in the course narrative from a reign or governorship. Labels identify shared imperial power, a deputy position, a territorial governorship or a non-ruling role. Durations are approximate learning aids, not exact calculations in months or inclusive ancient regnal-year counts. Augustus spans 27 BCE to 14 CE, about 40 years: there is no year zero. Antipas spans 4 BCE to 39 CE, about 42 elapsed years (inclusive regnal-year counts can differ).

Caracalla is shown as emperor including co-rule from 198 to 217, with the transition after his father's death in 211 explained. Constantine is shown from 306 to 337 with sole rule from 324 stated separately. Theodosius II shows 408–450 as eastern successor, while noting co-emperor status from 402. Julian's 361–363 is his sole reign, with proclamation in 360 distinguished. Herod's appointment in 40 BCE is distinguished from rule in 37 BCE; Agrippa's overall 37–44 from Judea in 41–44.

Quirinius's appointment connected with the census of 6 CE is securely identifiable, but no arbitrary complete tenure length is supplied. Quietus's short appointment in 117 and dismissal under Hadrian are distinguished from execution in 118. Cestius's uncertain start around 63, attested by 65, is marked. Pompey, Antony, Helena and Eudocia are explicitly identified as not reigning as emperors, rather than assigning them fictitious reign lengths.

Provenance links are stored with each entry and exposed in the person context and footer: Metropolitan Museum Roman and Byzantine ruler lists, British Museum, London Museum, Oxford Classical Dictionary, Livius and the Jewish Encyclopedia; the specialist Oxford University Press discussion of Nerva confirms his sixteen-month reign. These are supplemental external chronology checks, distinct from the original course sources.
